# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- **The ennakkovero card no longer publishes a payable balance** — the "still to pay" headline, its progress bar, and the "that surplus reduces the amount still to pay" banner have been removed, along with `remaining_to_pay_eur`, `year_balance_eur`, `overpaid_overall`, `over_under_eur`, `computed_for_paid_eur`, and `remaining_tax_eur` from `GET /transactions/tax-calculations/declaration-summary`. The app cannot see the tax account — instalments already charged, salary withholding, refunds, and reallocations are all invisible to it — so any balance it computed was a guess presented as fact, and the surplus banner actively advised paying less than Verohallinto had invoiced (an overpaid prepayment is not netted into a new prepayment decision; it is settled at the following year's verotuspäätös). The card now reports the year's computed advance tax, splits it into declared vs not-yet-declared (`undeclared_tax_eur`), and points at OmaVero for the actual balance. A regression test asserts the removed keys never come back.
- **Advance-tax calculations are scoped to the Fidelity ESPP account, not just to the symbol** — a new `app/services/espp_scope.py` is the single source of truth for what the tracker covers. `GET /transactions/tax-calculation` and the declaration summary now restrict FIFO lots and saved calculations to accounts of type `espp`, and reject any other symbol with a `400`. The `symbol` query parameter has been removed from the declaration summary. Previously lots were selected by symbol alone across every account, so any MSFT held at Nordnet would both corrupt the ESPP FIFO cost basis and be declared a second time even though the broker already reports it to Verohallinto.
- **Dividends no longer enter the ennakkovero bracket** — `compute_capital_income()` gained an `include_dividends` flag and the per-sale calculation passes `False`. Dividends are reported on the annual return rather than through the advance-tax application, so Verohallinto's ennakkovero basis is gains-only; counting 85 % of dividends there pushed a sale into the 34 % band earlier than the actual tax decision does. The standalone capital-income summary keeps its whole-portfolio, dividend-inclusive view, and the calculation notes now state plainly that other capital income still counts toward the same €30,000 limit in the final assessment.
- **Marking a sale declared no longer fabricates a payment** — the declared toggle used to fill `paid_amount_eur` with the computed tax and `paid_date` with today, which made untouched rows indistinguishable from genuinely paid ones and silently inflated the year's "paid" total. The toggle now records declaration status only; a payment amount and date must be entered explicitly in the sale's calculation dialog. Per-sale paid status is still tracked and shown.
- **Analysis is now a Portfolio Cockpit** — “Refresh portfolio” updates prices, portfolio health, current exceptions, stance, gross S&P 500 Total Return comparison, turnover, and tracked Finnish tax facts. The UI explicitly discloses that benchmark returns exclude cash, transaction fees, and taxes; it refuses to invent an investable after-tax ETF comparator and notes that ETFs cannot be held in a Finnish OST. News research is separate from portfolio guidance. Full model output, policy controls, immutable evidence, backtests, and history remain under Advanced evidence and tools.
- **Transaction provenance no longer invents missing history** — Nordnet/Fidelity note text is retained as provenance but no longer proves that the stored ledger is incomplete. Personal backtest eligibility now uses concrete FIFO non-negativity, current-holding reconciliation, supported tax treatment, and market-data coverage; the user's stored history remains authoritative unless one of those checks identifies a specific inconsistency. A Nordnet open-lot re-import may replace only a previous open-lot snapshot and now returns `409` instead of deleting richer history or replacing a newer snapshot. Fidelity statements merge by period and native transaction identity, preserve manual and earlier-period rows, and persist account-level snapshot recency so even a newer zero-holdings statement cannot later be overwritten by an older statement.
- **Universe backtests require an explicit licensed price file** — `BACKTEST_MARKET_DATA_PATH` points to long-form EUR adjusted-close data and is intentionally required for historical S&P 500 selection evidence. The engine refuses to substitute current-only Yahoo coverage for removed/delisted constituents and verifies benchmark rows against an independent NYSE session calendar.
- **AI analysis is now risk-constrained and shadow-only** — analysis and chat use typed portfolio snapshots, explicit benchmark/risk/cost limits, strict validated JSON, and deterministic abstention for missing or stale inputs. Claude may explain supplied calculations and risk candidates but cannot invent prices, tax impacts, expected alpha, downside, or executable trades; validated risk-only candidates are appended by application code rather than copied from model output. Informational news and tax-data triage remains available when trade eligibility is blocked, and shadow decisions never create alerts.
- **Model analysis is now optional and on-demand** — scheduled market/news collection and evaluation of existing shadow outcomes remain, but daily summaries and weekly model reviews no longer run automatically. Manual Advanced analyses still record immutable inputs and outputs, including complete model requests and news context under dedicated SHA-256 hashes.
- **Backend startup now applies Alembic before serving traffic** — the container upgrades the schema before Uvicorn, and application startup no longer calls `Base.metadata.create_all()`. The first migration-enabled release adopts an unversioned database only when all known legacy tables are present; partial schemas fail closed. A failed migration prevents the new revision from becoming healthy instead of creating tables without advancing `alembic_version`.
- **AI model upgraded to Claude Sonnet 5** — both the scheduled analysis (`app/services/analysis.py`) and the streaming chat (`app/routers/chat.py`) now call `claude-sonnet-5`. Sonnet 5 replaces the old extended-thinking parameters (`thinking.type="enabled"` + `budget_tokens` + `temperature=1`) with `thinking.type="adaptive"` + `output_config.effort="high"`; the previous parameters raise an HTTP 400 on this model.
- **Holdings shown in native listed currency** — the holdings table now renders each position's Price and Avg Cost in the stock's own listed currency (USD for MSFT, EUR for SAMPO, …) instead of mixing EUR and USD in one row. New `avg_cost_basis_native` / `total_cost_native` columns are tracked per lot through the FIFO replay and added automatically on startup (Postgres `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`).
- **Container App runs always-on** — the backend now runs at `min_replicas: 1` (single always-on replica) to eliminate 10–20 s cold starts; the earlier scale-to-zero + keep-alive-cron approach has been retired (no keep-alive workflow remains in the repo).
- **Finnish-locale dates across the UI** — a shared `formatDate()` helper (pinned to `fi-FI`, `d.m.yyyy`) replaces the previous ISO `yyyy-mm-dd` formatting in the Record-Trade card, alerts, analysis, earnings calendar, and performance chart.
- **Yahoo-style Today P/L column** — the holdings table's Today column shows the primary day change in a tinted pill with the extended-hours (pre/post-market) change on a readable second line, instead of two cramped near-identical numbers.
- **Per-sale ennakkovero calculator is now MSFT-only in the UI** — the tax-calculation button on the Transaction History page now appears only for `MSFT` sell rows (a `TAX_FILING_SYMBOLS` frontend constant mirroring the backend `tax_filing_required` gate). MSFT is the only holding that requires a manual Finnish ennakkovero filing (it is a Fidelity ESPP position reported in the US); every other holding sits at Nordnet, which calculates and withholds Finnish capital-gains tax automatically, so showing a manual calculator for them was misleading.
- **Redesigned the bracket section of the tax dialog** — replaced the inline 30 %/34 % note with a dedicated `BracketCard`: a stacked bracket bar (prior YTD income · this sale's 30 % part · this sale's 34 % part) with a dashed €30,000 threshold marker, a color-coded breakdown listing each part with its own tax amount, the total ennakkovero for the sale, and a green headroom line when the sale stays inside the 30 % band. The header text states plainly whether the whole sale, part of it, or none of it falls into the 34 % bracket.
- **Frontend hosting** — migrated React frontend from Azure Container Apps (nginx) to Azure Static Web Apps (Free tier). The frontend now calls the backend cross-origin via the `VITE_API_BASE_URL` build-time variable; the auth cookie uses `SameSite=None; Secure`. Cuts hosting cost by ~€19/month and adds a global CDN. CI builds with `VITE_API_BASE_URL` from a GitHub repo Variable and deploys via `azure/static-web-apps-deploy@v1`. The old `portfolio-frontend` Container App, its nginx proxy, and the frontend Dockerfile have been removed.

### Added
- **"Ennakkoveron muutos OmaVerossa" block on the per-sale tax dialog** — `GET /transactions/tax-calculation` now returns an `omavero_submission` object with the cumulative year-to-date ESPP `luovutushinnat` / `hankintamenot` / `luovutusvoitot` and the advance-tax increase those figures should produce. Changing the ennakkovero in OmaVero takes the cumulative annual gain, not a per-sale figure, so showing only the sale's own tax left no way to know what to type in or to check the resulting decision. The cumulative totals are built from the transaction ledger rather than from saved calculations (which can be stale or missing), and a sale that is not yet recorded is added on top instead of being double counted.
- **Saved tax calculations record which engine produced them** — new nullable `engine_version` column on `tax_calculations` (Alembic revision `a1b2c3d4e5f6`), stamped on save and surfaced as `engine_version` / `is_legacy` on the API and as a "legacy" badge plus a card-level warning in the UI. The tax engine was corrected in June 2026 (per-lot hankintameno-olettama, USD→EUR conversion on import, calendar-based 10-year boundary), which silently changed the tax on a sale that had already been declared and paid. Existing rows read as `NULL` and are reported as legacy rather than being back-stamped.
- **Dual-track, pre-registered alpha backtesting** — `alpha-momentum-trend-v1` locks a monthly 12-minus-1 benchmark-relative momentum and 200-day trend hypothesis before results are observed. One engine simulates an 80% S&P 500 Total Return core plus a 20% point-in-time constituent sleeve; the second reconstructs split-normalized FIFO acquisition cohorts, preserves 80% of each actual cohort, and applies the signal only to the 20% active sleeve. A pinned MIT-licensed membership interval ledger, specification/universe/market/transaction hashes, fixed development-validation-holdout splits, moving-block confidence intervals, delisted-price fail-closed checks, immutable `backtest_runs`, and dashboard controls make assumptions and blockers inspectable. Deferred-tax evidence can run; standard taxable evidence fails closed because the locked adjusted-close input has no point-in-time distributions for Finnish dividend tax. New endpoints live under `/analysis/backtests`; Alembic revision `c9d6e1f4b3a8` adds the run ledger and `d0f1a2b3c4d5` persists holdings-snapshot recency.
- **Deterministic risk-enforcement candidates** — the weekly shadow review can now calculate sell-to-cash plans for explicit single-position and sector breaches. The engine maximizes retained invested value under both limits, uses recorded FIFO lots and Finnish deemed-cost rules, prefers supported lower-tax-drag accounts, includes explicit transaction-cost and tax estimates, and fails closed when turnover conflicts, tax lots are unsupported, limits are infeasible, or drawdown/volatility/tracking-error breaches lack marginal-risk inputs. Risk-only outcomes are measured but never counted toward the alpha promotion gate.
- **AI proof-of-value scorecard and shadow ledger** — the Analysis page now configures the investment horizon, S&P 500 Total Return benchmark, drawdown, volatility, tracking-error, concentration, turnover, transaction-cost, and minimum-net-alpha contract; displays deterministic risk/data-quality checks; and reports immutable run reproducibility and net value-add evidence without claiming alpha prematurely. New endpoints: `GET/PUT /analysis/policy`, `GET /analysis/proof-of-value`, `GET /analysis/shadow-runs`, and `GET /analysis/proof-of-value-report`. Alembic revision `a7d4e9c2f1b6` adds analysis runs, shadow recommendations, and recommendation outcomes; `b8c5d0e3a2f7` reconciles legacy tables, holding columns, and complete analysis-input records that startup previously left outside migration history.
- **Edit & delete trades** — the Transaction History page gained a pencil (edit) and trash (delete) action on every row. Editing opens a full form (date, quantity, type, symbol, account, price with EUR/USD toggle + trade-date FX override, commission with its own currency toggle, notes, live EUR preview); saving recomputes the EUR derivatives and replays FIFO holdings. `PATCH /transactions/{id}` now accepts all editable fields and `DELETE /transactions/{id}` removes a trade, both auto-recomputing affected holdings.
- **Commission currency toggle on trades** — the Record-Trade card records commission in EUR or USD independently of the trade currency (e.g. a USD stock purchase with an EUR-denominated Nordnet commission), converting with the same trade-date FX rate as the price/share.
- **Ennakkovero declaration & payment tracking (MSFT)** — saved per-sale tax calculations can now be marked **declared & paid** in OmaVero, with the **actual amount and date** the user paid recorded (`declared_at`, `paid_amount_eur`, `paid_date` on `tax_calculations`). The per-row tax-statement icon is now a traffic light: **muted** (no calculation), **amber** (saved but not yet declared/paid), **green** (declared & paid). A new **"Ennakkovero — declared vs remaining (MSFT)"** card on the Transaction History page shows, per year, the **total** advance tax across the year's MSFT sales split into **declared** vs **still-to-declare**, a progress bar, an **OmaVero annual summary** (total luovutushinnat, hankintamenot, luovutusvoitot/-tappiot), a per-sale list with a one-click declared toggle and an **expandable per-sale OmaVero panel** (luovutushinta, hankintameno, luovutusvoitto/-tappio, ennakkovero) so the return can be filled without opening each calculation, and a **paid-vs-computed reconciliation** that flags any over/under-payment versus the (possibly corrected) computed figure. The **"still to pay"** headline is **payment-aware**: it nets what you've **actually paid** against the year's total liability (`remaining_to_pay = total_tax − total_paid`), so an overpayment on an earlier sale reduces what's left to pay on later sales rather than charging the raw computed tax on top — you pay exactly enough to even out the year, no excess. If you've paid more than the whole year's liability the card shows the over-coverage (refunded at the year-end assessment). Because the per-sale figures are marginal and stack chronologically, they sum exactly to the year's total prepayment, so "remaining" is mathematically correct. The sale's tax dialog gains a declaration panel for entering the exact paid amount/date. New `PATCH /transactions/tax-calculations/{id}/declaration` and `GET /transactions/tax-calculations/declaration-summary?year=&symbol=` endpoints; summary aggregation is a pure, unit-tested service (`app/services/declarations.py`). New nullable columns are applied automatically on startup (Postgres `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`) and via Alembic migration `e5c1a7f3b2d8`.
- **Automatic 30 %/34 % bracket in the per-sale ennakkovero calculation**— the tax calculationfor a sold stock (`GET /transactions/tax-calculation`) now automatically positions the sale's gain on the per-year €30,000 capital-income bracket using the user's capital income realised **earlier in the same calendar year** — i.e. gains and 85 %-taxable dividends dated **strictly before this sale** (OST excluded), computed directly from stored transactions, no manual re-entry. Chronological stacking means the first sales of the year fill the 30 % band first and later sales cross into 34 % once the year-to-date total passes €30,000. The tax math is marginal: `bracket_total_tax(prior + gain) − bracket_total_tax(prior)`, so a net year-to-date capital **loss** correctly shields the gain and an already-over-threshold year taxes the whole sale at 34 %. A hypothetical (unsaved) QuickTrade sale naturally stacks on top of all income before its date. The response gains a `bracket` object (prior YTD income, threshold, headroom, and the 30 %/34 % split of this sale), and the dialog shows a prominent amber warning when part or all of the sale is taxed at 34 % (or a green "stays in the 30 % band, €X headroom left" note otherwise). Untracked capital income (rental, interest, non-listed dividends) still counts toward the same €30k and is caveated in the notes.
- **Year-to-date capital-income tracker (€30k bracket)**— a new card on the Transaction History page shows how much **taxable capital income** has accumulated in the year versus the €30,000 Finnish capital-income bracket threshold, so it's easy to see how close the year is to the higher 34 % rate. It is computed **directly from stored transactions** (no saved tax calculations required) by a new pure, unit-tested service (`app/services/capital_income.py`) behind `GET /transactions/capital-income-summary?year=`. Realized capital gains use the same per-lot best-of hankintameno-olettama engine as the ennakkovero calculation, with **per-account FIFO** cost basis. **Dividends** are now included at **85 % taxable** (TVL 33a §, 15 % tax-free); accumulating ETFs (e.g. SXR8) have no dividend rows so they're naturally excluded. **OST accounts (`tax_treatment = deferred`)** are excluded entirely — both gains and dividends are taxed only on withdrawal — and the card surfaces the excluded OST amounts. Capital losses net against the combined income before the 30 %/34 % bracket is applied. The card shows a progress bar, remaining 30 %-band headroom (or amount over the threshold), a gains / taxable-dividends / estimated-tax breakdown, and a year selector. (Replaces the earlier saved-calculation-only `GET /transactions/tax-calculations/summary` endpoint.) Note: rental, interest, non-listed dividends and other untracked capital income also count toward the same €30k limit but are not included.
- **Delete / re-run saved tax calculations** — added the ability to remove saved ennakkovero calculations so they can be re-run after a logic correction. New `DELETE /transactions/tax-calculations/{id}` (single) and `DELETE /transactions/tax-calculations/` (bulk, optional `symbol`/`year` filters) endpoints. The Transaction History page now shows a per-row trash icon next to each saved sell calculation and a "Delete tax calcs (N)" button in the header (both confirm first). Saving is now **idempotent**: re-running the same sale replaces its previous calculation (matched by linked transaction, else by symbol + sell date + quantity) instead of creating a duplicate row.
- **Automatic USD→EUR FX conversion on import** — Fidelity ESPP statements are USD-native, so imported transactions previously kept raw USD figures in their EUR fields until the user manually ran `POST /transactions/fix-fx-rates/{symbol}`. The Fidelity upload now converts every transaction to EUR automatically using the historical ECB reference rate for each transaction's own date (frankfurter.app), so cost basis and the ennakkovero calculation are tax-ready immediately. The conversion is extracted into a reusable, unit-tested service (`app/services/fx.py`); the manual endpoint now reuses it as a re-run/fallback. The conversion is best-effort — an FX-API outage logs a warning and leaves the import intact (re-run the endpoint later). The upload response includes an `fx_conversion` summary.
- **Cookie-based password gate** — simple shared-secret auth replacing MSAL for personal use. No more Azure AD token acquisition on page load; login is a single password prompt stored as an HTTP-only cookie (with a Bearer-token fallback for mobile browsers that block cross-site cookies).

### Fixed
- **A failed backend deploy no longer reports success** — the workflow now waits for the Container App revision built from the pushed commit to become healthy, hold 100 % of traffic, and answer `/health`, dumping container logs and failing the run otherwise; the Static Web Apps job likewise confirms the site serves a built bundle. Azure keeps serving the previous revision when a new one cannot start and `azure/container-apps-deploy-action` returns success regardless, so a broken Alembic upgrade left production running a weeks-old image while every run stayed green. Also dropped the invalid `skip_api_build` input the runner had been warning about.
- **Core portfolio refresh no longer depends on Anthropic** — the primary cockpit uses a dedicated deterministic endpoint, so a missing API key cannot produce a partially completed portfolio refresh or silently leave the visible cards unchanged.
- **Backtest evidence now fails closed on sparse calendars, stock splits, and incomplete tax inputs** — benchmark coverage is checked against every expected NYSE session instead of the CSV's self-defined dates; personal FIFO quantities and per-share prices are normalized with hashed Yahoo split events before reconciliation; turnover uses each trade's contemporaneous portfolio value; and taxable scenarios are blocked rather than treating gross dividend reinvestment as after-tax return. Fidelity statement re-imports also merge overlapping periods without deleting manual/earlier history or replacing newer holdings snapshots.
- **Acquisition commissions are included in FIFO tax basis** — buy-side fees are allocated across each acquisition lot and now flow consistently through holding cost recalculation, realized gains, per-sale tax calculations, capital-income totals, and AI proof-of-value snapshots. Sell-side fees remain deducted from proceeds.
- **Shadow-output guards cover passive advice and fabricated figures** — inflected recommendations such as “recommend selling,” passive instructions such as “should be sold,” and numerical claims absent from the supplied snapshot, policy, tax rules, or news are rejected before structured analysis or chat reaches the user.
- **S&P 500 comparison is equity-only** — crypto accounts and symbols flagged as crypto are excluded from the performance calculation and its data-quality warnings, so the chart compares only stock and ETF holdings against the S&P 500 Total Return index. The beating/trailing badge now displays the return gap with a `%` suffix instead of `pp`.
- **Performance chart no longer fails on irrelevant or incomplete legacy positions** — finite-period comparisons now include current holding rows (including split-sensitive zero-quantity snapshots) and positions traded inside the selected window, while fully closed older positions are excluded even when an unrelated Yahoo ticker happens to share their local symbol. In-window transaction histories that cannot reconcile to the current holdings snapshot are omitted with an explicit warning instead of blocking the entire chart. Broker-recorded bonus-share adjustments are no longer counted again on top of Yahoo split normalization, and duplicate total-ratio/bonus-ratio split events are collapsed. The frontend no longer shows a misleading legacy-benchmark methodology while the request is in an error state.
- **Portfolio vs S&P 500 performance comparison** — the chart now compares the invested holdings' EUR time-weighted market return against the dividend-inclusive S&P 500 Total Return index in EUR. Raw closes determine actual cross-security market-value weights, while adjusted-close relatives capture dividends and splits; both series share the same calendar/FX basis and begin at a visible 0% baseline. Position changes no longer create artificial jumps, holding and transaction quantities are normalized to the same split basis, and importer statement dates are persisted on holdings so late uploads do not shift history. Missing, stale, inconsistent, or truncated market data now fails explicitly instead of producing a plausible but incomplete chart. The UI discloses that cash, fees, and taxes are excluded. Fingerprinted cache entries update atomically, so imports, edits, deletes, and concurrent cold requests cannot leave stale or conflicting results; incomplete opening balances are disclosed instead of silently presented as exact history.
- **Analysis no longer lost when alert generation fails** — the four analysis endpoints now commit the saved analysis *before* attempting to generate alerts (wrapped in try/except), and `create_alert()` defensively truncates over-long titles to the 500-char column limit. Previously a too-long Claude-generated alert title raised `StringDataRightTruncationError`, rolling back the whole transaction and discarding the analysis.
- **Trade-date EUR/USD FX rate** — the Record-Trade card now re-fetches the EUR/USD rate when the trade date changes (the React Query key previously ignored the date, so it always used today's rate). Historical USD transactions were backfilled with the correct per-date rate and holdings recomputed via FIFO.
- **Dividend auto-detection no longer books unpaid dividends early** — the yfinance dividend detector (`app/services/dividend_detector.py`) keyed off the **ex-dividend date** and recorded a dividend as income as soon as that date passed, even though the cash is not paid until weeks later (yfinance exposes only the ex-date, never the pay date). This booked declared-but-unpaid dividends prematurely — e.g. MSFT's 2026 Q2 dividend ($0.91/share, ex-date 2026-05-21, **payable 2026-06-11**) was recorded on 2026-05-21. The detector now estimates the pay date as `ex-date + PAYMENT_DELAY_DAYS` (21 days, ≈ the US large-cap ex→pay gap) and only records a dividend once that estimated pay date is on or before today, storing the transaction at the estimated pay date so the income falls in the correct tax year. The recognition window (`LOOKBACK_DAYS`) was widened from 30 to 60 days so the daily scheduler reliably catches each dividend once its pay date arrives, and deduplication now matches within a ±7-day window (absorbing the pay-date approximation and small date differences against manually imported rows). Auto-detected dividend rows now store `quantity = 0` (a dividend is income, not a share movement) with the per-share figure and share count preserved in the notes. The decision logic is extracted into a pure, unit-tested module (`app/services/dividend_logic.py`).
- **Capital-income tracker now ignores dividend-withholding rows** — the Nordnet importer historically mapped both `OSINKO` (gross dividend, +) and `ENNAKKOPIDÄTYS` (advance withholding, −) to the `dividend` transaction type, so naively summing dividend rows yielded a **net** figure. The €30k tracker (`app/services/capital_income.py`) now skips non-positive dividend rows (withholding is prepaid tax, a foreign-tax credit, not income), so it counts the **gross** listed-share dividend at 85 %. The importer (`import_nordnet_history.py`) no longer imports `ENNAKKOPIDÄTYS` as a dividend; the Fidelity importer (`import_fidelity_history.py`) now also emits a gross `dividend` income row alongside each DRIP reinvestment `buy` (MSFT dividends are auto-reinvested, so the taxable income was previously invisible — recorded as 0-share rows that don't affect cost basis). Verified against Fidelity statements (e.g. 2026 Q1 MSFT $541.31 gross, $81.20 US withholding) and Nordnet CSVs (`OSINKO` Summa = qty × per-share = gross).
- **Restored the `GET /by-transaction/{id}` tax-calculation route** — the decorator on `get_tax_calculation_by_transaction` was accidentally dropped when the DELETE endpoints were added, leaving the function unregistered (the endpoint would 404). The decorator is restored. (No live UI used it, so there was no user-visible breakage.)
- **"Failed to delete" error on a successful delete** — the frontend API helper unconditionally parsed the response body as JSON, but `DELETE /transactions/tax-calculations/{id}` returns `204 No Content` (empty body). Deleting a saved tax calculation succeeded on the backend but threw a JSON-parse error in the UI, showing a misleading "Failed to delete" toast. `request()` now returns early for `204`/empty bodies.
- **QuickTrade USD-before-rate guard** — submitting a USD trade via the Enter key could bypass the disabled submit button and pass the raw USD price through as EUR (the `priceEur` fallback) when the sale-day EUR/USD rate hadn't loaded yet, inflating the cost basis / ennakkovero. `handleSubmit` now blocks USD trades (and USD fees) until the rate is available and shows a "waiting for exchange rate" toast.
- **Ennakkovero 10-year boundary now calendar-based**— the hankintameno-olettama rate (40 % for lots held ≥10 years, 20 % otherwise) is now determined by calendar arithmetic (the lot's exact 10-year anniversary date) instead of a `days / 365.25` approximation, which could misclassify a lot by ~1 day right at the boundary and flip the deemed-cost rate. Leap-day lots (Feb 29) fall back to Feb 28 in a non-leap target year. Extracted into pure helpers `ten_year_anniversary()` / `held_at_least_10_years()` in `app/services/tax.py` with regression tests (`tests/test_holding_period.py`).
- **Ennakkovero guidance notes corrected** — replaced the incorrect "file an ennakkoveroilmoitus within 2 months of the sale" note with the correct lisäennakko (additional prepayment) guidance: pay in MyTax without interest by the end of January following the tax year (e.g. a 2026 sale → by 31 Jan 2027), minimum €170, with relief interest accruing thereafter. Added notes surfacing the €1,000 small-disposals exemption (TVL 48.6 §, a per-year aggregate the calculator cannot see) and clarifying that the 30 %/34 % bracket depends on the taxpayer's whole-year capital income (dividends, rental, other gains) and deductible losses — verified against the Finnish Tax Administration's guidance and the KPMG "Taxation of the Stock Awards in FI" memo.
- **Ennakkovero per-lot hankintameno-olettama** — the capital-gains tax calculation(`/transactions/tax-calculation`) now applies the deemed acquisition cost (hankintameno-olettama) **per lot** by each lot's own holding period (40 % for lots held ≥10 years, 20 % otherwise) and lets each lot independently choose the cheaper of actual vs. deemed cost. Previously a single recent lot forced the whole sale to the 20 % rate (`all_over_10` bug), overstating the taxable gain and overpaying tax (~€2,400 on a representative mixed-lot MSFT sale). Added a lot-coverage guard: if recorded buy lots cover fewer shares than were sold, the shortfall shares now get the 20 % olettama and a loud warning instead of a silently understated cost basis. The math is extracted into a pure, unit-tested module (`app/services/tax.py`) with regression tests (`tests/test_tax.py`). Output adds `hankintameno_kaytetty` (effective deduction), a `coverage` block, and per-lot `applied_deemed_rate`/`method`; `recommended_method` may now be `yhdistelma` (mixed).
- **FIFO cost basis** — switched P/L calculation from average cost to FIFO (First In, First Out), matching Fidelity and Finnish tax rules. Previously, selling old cheap ESPP lots didn't properly remove their low cost basis, overstating unrealized P/L by ~€21k.
- **Dashboard 500 error** — removed shadowed `JSONResponse` import that caused the combined dashboard endpoint to crash.
- **Mobile card truncation** — summary cards (PortfolioSummary, DailySummary, CashAvailable) no longer truncate values on narrow viewports.
- **Stale earnings calendar** — cached earnings data now filters out past dates at response time, and frontend guards against negative `daysUntil` values. Previously, 24h-cached data could show yesterday's earnings after midnight.

### Changed
- **Auth simplification** — removed MSAL authentication entirely; the app now uses a lightweight cookie-based password gate. This eliminates the Azure AD app registration dependency and speeds up page load significantly.
